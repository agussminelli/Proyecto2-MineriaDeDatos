Input:
    Research dataset (Train + Dev)
    Minimum class frequency = 10
    Evaluation fraction = 0.20
    Random seeds

1. Find causes occurring at least 10 times at EVERY site.

2. For each selected cause:
    quota[cause] = minimum site-specific count

3. For each random seed:
    For each site and cause:
        a. Randomly sample quota[cause] narratives
        b. Reserve approximately 20% for evaluation
        c. Keep the remaining 80% as training candidates

4. For each target site:
    UNSEEN:
        Use training candidates from the other 5 sites

    SEEN:
        Include training candidates from the target site
        Replace an equal number from the other 5 sites
        Preserve the number of samples per cause

    EVALUATION:
        Use exactly the same target-site examples
        for UNSEEN and SEEN

5. Return both training sets and their shared evaluation set.
